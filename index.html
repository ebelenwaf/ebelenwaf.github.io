About

Hello! I am an Associate Professor at the Department of Computing Sciences, Villanova University. My research interests span machine learning and natural language processing, information visualization, health informatics, data provenance, and IoT / cyber-physical systems security.

I graduated with my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Howard University under the supervision of Dr. Gedare Bloom, where my dissertation explored applications of data provenance to the Internet of Things.
