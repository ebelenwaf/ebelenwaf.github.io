Fostering Digital Inclusion for Low-Resource Nigerian Languages: A Case Study of Igbo and Nigerian Pidgin
Ebelechukwu Nwafor, Minh Phuc Nguyen
Proceedings of the Eighth Workshop on Technologies for Machine Translation of Low-Resource Languages (LoResMT 2025)
PDF
Current state-of-the-art large language models perform exceptionally well for high-resource languages but often lack accuracy for low-resource African languages such as Igbo and Nigerian Pidgin. This study creates benchmark datasets for Igbo-English and Nigerian Pidgin-English translation tasks and evaluates GPT-based models alongside other state-of-the-art translation models specifically designed for low-resource languages.

Paul Agbaje, Arkajyoti Mitra, Afia Anjum, Pranali Khose, Ebelechukwu Nwafor, Habeeb Olufowobi
arXiv preprint, 2025
arXiv

Evaluating Large Language Models for Enhanced Intrusion Detection in Internet of Things Networks
Ebelechukwu Nwafor, Ujjwal Baskota, Md. Salik Parwez, Jeremy Blackstone, Habeeb Olufowobi
IEEE Global Communications Conference (GLOBECOM), 2024
This paper explores the application of Large Language Models to network intrusion detection in IoT settings, an area that remains largely unexplored despite LLMs gaining significant traction. We fine-tune GPT-3.5 and develop an embedding similarity technique using the CIC IoT 2023 dataset.
